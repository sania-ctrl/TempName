# AM400 Recoater and Powder System

## Recoater Blade

The recoater blade spreads a thin, uniform layer of metal powder across the build plate before each
layer is fused by the laser. The blade must be inspected for wear before every build; a damaged blade
causes uneven powder distribution and porosity defects in the finished part.

## Build Plate

The build plate is the platform on which the part is built, layer by layer. It is heated to reduce
thermal stress and must be leveled before starting a build. The recoater blade travels directly across
the build plate on each pass.

## Small Powder Bottle

The small powder bottle stores unused metal powder during operation and is refilled from the main
powder hopper. It connects to the powder delivery system that feeds the recoater.

# AM400 Maintenance

## Wiper Cleaning

Wiper cleaning requires a 99% alcohol concentration to ensure complete removal of residual metal
particles from the wiper surface. Using a lower concentration risks leaving particle residue that can
contaminate subsequent builds.

## Chiller

The chiller regulates the temperature of the laser optics and the build chamber. It must be checked
weekly for coolant level and connected to the machine via the safe change filter assembly.

## Safe Change Filter Valve

The upper safe change filter valve, Valve (F1), is located on the KF flange of the safe change filter
assembly. During maintenance, the upper isolating valve is opened to fill the filter housing with water
before the filter cartridge is replaced.
